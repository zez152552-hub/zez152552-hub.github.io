World news in 30 seconds.

Global Pulse publishes short vertical videos explaining one world event at a time: what happened, where, and why it matters, with the sources shown on screen.

How it works

  • Every story is checked against at least two independent news outlets, which are credited on screen and in the description.
  • We never re-upload footage or photos from news agencies. Backgrounds are maps we draw ourselves or freely licensed photos (public domain or Creative Commons), always credited.
  • Videos are produced on our own computer and published to our own channels through the official YouTube Data API and TikTok Content Posting API.

About the Global Pulse Uploader app

"Global Pulse Uploader" is a private tool used only by the Global Pulse team to upload our own videos to our own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

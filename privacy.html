Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how the "Global Pulse Uploader" application ("the App") handles data. The App is a private publishing tool operated by Global Pulse to upload Global Pulse's own videos to Global Pulse's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

1. Who uses the App

Only the Global Pulse team. The App is not distributed to the public and is never used to sign in third-party users.

2. Data the App accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes, the App uploads videos to the authorized Global Pulse channel and reads the channel name to confirm the connection.
  • TikTok: with the user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish scopes, the App uploads videos to the authorized Global Pulse TikTok account and checks their publishing status.

The App does not read comments, messages, subscribers, viewers' data or any other account content.

3. Storage

Access and refresh tokens are stored only on the Global Pulse computer that runs the App, in local files that are never shared, sold or sent to any other server. No analytics, advertising or tracking tools are used.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the upload feature described above, in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploaded videos are also governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions and from TikTok's "Manage app permissions" settings. Deleting the local token files also removes the App's access.
